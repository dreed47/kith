Kith

A private family history website for your relatives: people, families, photos, documents and stories, with living people kept private. Free and open source. You run it on your own server.

Install Code on GitHub

What it does

  • Person and family pages with life events, sources with scans of the record, notes, a life story, and a map of where a life happened.
  • Charts: ancestors, descendants, hourglass, and a fan chart with photos that prints as a poster.
  • Census by year, with suggestions that match each name in a census transcription to a person in the tree.
  • Photos and documents, with faces tagged to people, and comments.
  • Privacy: living people are hidden from those who may not see them. Relatives can ask for an account.
  • Editing on the site: add relatives, merge duplicates, tidy place names, and data checks for impossible dates.
  • GEDCOM import (with undo) and export.
  • Five themes, including dark and large print. Works on phones.

Install

On a Proxmox VE host's shell, one command makes a Debian container with Kith, MariaDB and the first admin account:

bash -c "$(curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/dreed47/kith/main/ct/kith.sh)"

Docker and by-hand Debian installs are in the README. Put Kith behind HTTPS before it's on the internet.

About

Kith runs a real family's site every day. It's a "here's the code" project: pull requests are welcome, but there are no support promises.

It's built by a veteran, retired IT professional working with AI (Anthropic's Claude). A person decides what gets built and reviews and tests every change, and the tests cover every line.
