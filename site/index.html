Leave the cloud

Your family isn't a product

Cloud family tree sites can raise prices, change their terms, get bought, or shut down. For many of them, your family's data is the business. With Kith, nobody gets your data unless you give it to them.

A cloud family tree site

  • A subscription that never ends, and goes up
  • Your relatives' details on someone else's servers
  • Terms that change, and owners that change
  • Ads, tracking and upsells
  • Stop paying and lose the records and tools your work depends on

Kith

  • Free and open source, with no account to pay for
  • Runs on your own server, at home or anywhere you choose
  • You decide who gets in, and what they can see
  • No ads, no trackers, no analytics. Maps use OpenStreetMap, and one daily version check (a random ID and the version number, nothing else) can be turned off. Every connection, listed
  • Export a GEDCOM file any time. The code is yours to keep, too

Why Kith

Made for the whole family, not just the researcher

Most genealogy software is built for the one person doing the research. Kith is built for everyone that person wants to share it with.

Private by default

Living people are hidden from anyone who isn't allowed to see them. Relatives ask for an account, you say yes, and they can mark "this is me".

Easy for everyone

Clear pages that work on phones. Five themes, including dark and large print. A weekly email of what's new keeps the family coming back.

Serious about sources

Citations with scans of the record, census transcriptions matched to people, and data checks that catch impossible dates before your cousins do.

Charts

See the family at a glance

Every chart is a click from any person, and every box links to that person's page.

  • Hourglass: parents above, children below, grouped by marriage
  • Ancestors, descendants, and a fan chart with photos
  • Print the fan chart as a poster for the next reunion
  • How any two people are related, as a row of faces

Research

The census, sorted out for you

Browse any census year by place and household. Kith reads the names typed into your census transcriptions and suggests who each one is. You say yes, and it can fill in a birth year from the age.

  • Places drill down from state to county to town
  • A map of where each life happened, in the order it happened
  • One-click searches on FamilySearch, Find a Grave, Ancestry and old newspapers

Looks

Light, dark, or large print

Each person picks the theme they like. Grandma gets large print, the night owls get dark, and nobody has to change a setting for anyone else.

Coming from TNG?

Kith grew out of TNG and keeps its table layout, so your whole site moves over in one command: people, families, sources, places, media records, and every user account with its password. Relatives log in as before, and old TNG links still find the right page.

Install

Up and running in minutes

On a Proxmox VE host, one command builds a container with Kith, its database and your first admin account.

Proxmox host shell 
$ bash -c "$(curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/dreed47/kith/main/ct/kith.sh)"
# Then your admin login is in the container: cat ~/kith.creds

Docker

Clone the repo and run docker compose up -d. A made-up sample family is included to try. Steps

Who's behind it

Kith is built by a veteran, retired IT professional, and it runs that family's own site every day. It's written with AI (Anthropic's Claude). A person decides what gets built, and reviews and tests every change.

It's a "here's the code" project: pull requests are welcome, but there are no support promises.
